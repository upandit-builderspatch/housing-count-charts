About These Charts

Data from HUD CHAS (Comprehensive Housing Affordability Strategy) 2018-2022.

Charts show affordable rental homes vs. renter households across three income tiers:

  • Extremely Low Income (≤30% AMI)
  • Very Low Income (≤50% AMI)
  • Low Income (≤80% AMI)
